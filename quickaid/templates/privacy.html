Privacy and your data

Health information is personal. Here is exactly what Quick Aid stores, for how long, and what you can do about it.

What is stored

  • Your checks: what you described, the result (including the AI's explanations and advice), and any personal notes you add.
  • Your chats about those results.
  • Your account: username, email, a hashed password, and your region and retention choice.
  • Not stored: your photos. A photo is checked, analysed and deleted immediately, even if the analysis fails. Only the written result is kept. If anything is ever left behind by a crash, a clean-up deletes it within {{ policy.uploaded_photos.leftover_sweep_minutes }} minutes.

How it is protected

  • Your symptoms, results, notes and chats are encrypted in the database, so a copy of the database file does not expose them.
  • Everything is tied to your account. Another user cannot open, search, change or delete your entries, even by guessing a link.
  • Pages that show your health information are never stored by your browser's cache.

Is anything sent to an AI service?

Only if you agree, and only to Google's Gemini API. Until you choose, nothing you type or upload leaves this server: a simple built-in basic mode works on the server itself. You can change your choice at any time under Account → Your data, and it applies to your very next request.

  • Google processes that data under its own terms for the API plan this service uses, which can differ between plans (for example in whether data may be used to improve Google's products). Ask the operator of this service which plan is in use before you rely on it.
  • The AI can be wrong. Our own safety rules always run on top of its answer and can only make advice more cautious, never less.
  • Emergency wording in what you type is recognised on this server before anything is sent, so urgent guidance never waits for, or depends on, the AI.
  • We do not sell your data and show no advertising. Usage statistics (timings and counts) contain no health text.

Your controls

  • Review every check in History, with search, filters and a full printable report.
  • Add notes to any check, for your own records or to share with a doctor.
  • Export everything you have saved, as JSON or a CSV summary.
  • Delete a single check, all of your history, or your whole account, at any time. It takes effect immediately.
  • Choose how long things are kept.

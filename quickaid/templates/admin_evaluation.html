AI evaluation

Internal · admins only

How well does the medical AI work, and is it getting better or worse? Evaluation runs score the system on a curated dataset with known answers, which is the only way to get recall and false-negative / false-positive rates. Live telemetry measures what really happens (failures, validity, latency, cost) but cannot tell right from wrong, because nobody labels real conversations.

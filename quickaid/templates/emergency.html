Call {{ emergency_number }} straight away for

  • Chest pain, pressure or tightness
  • Severe trouble breathing
  • Signs of stroke: face drooping, arm weakness, slurred speech
  • Bleeding that won't stop after 10 minutes of firm pressure
  • Loss of consciousness or a seizure
  • Severe burns, or burns to the face, hands or genitals
  • Poisoning or overdose
  • A suspected broken bone with deformity or numbness
  • Severe allergic reaction with swelling of the lips or throat
  • Thoughts of ending your life. In India you can also call Tele-MANAS on 14416

First-aid basics

Heavy bleeding

  1. Press firmly on the wound with a clean cloth. Don't lift it to check.
  2. If blood soaks through, add more cloth on top and keep pressing.
  3. Raise the injured limb above the heart if you can.
  4. Keep pressing until help arrives.

Call {{ emergency_number }} if it won't stop.

Burns

  1. Cool under cool running water for 20 minutes.
  2. Take off rings and tight items before swelling starts, unless stuck to the skin.
  3. Cover loosely with clean cling film or a non-fluffy cloth.
  4. Don't use ice, butter, toothpaste or burst blisters.

Call {{ emergency_number }} for large, deep or facial burns.

Choking (adult, can't speak or cough)

  1. Give up to 5 firm back blows between the shoulder blades.
  2. Then up to 5 abdominal thrusts (Heimlich).
  3. Alternate until the object comes out or the person collapses.

Call {{ emergency_number }} if they collapse and start CPR.

Not breathing: CPR

  1. Call {{ emergency_number }} or ask someone else to.
  2. Push hard and fast in the centre of the chest, about 100–120 times a minute.
  3. Press down about 5–6 cm. Let the chest rise fully each time.
  4. Don't stop until help or an AED arrives.

Stroke: think FAST

  • Face drooping on one side
  • Arm weak or numb
  • Speech slurred or confused
  • Time to call right now. Note when symptoms began.

Seizure

  1. Clear the area and cushion the head. Don't hold the person down.
  2. Never put anything in their mouth.
  3. Time it. Once it stops, roll them onto their side.

Call {{ emergency_number }} if it lasts over 5 minutes or it's their first.

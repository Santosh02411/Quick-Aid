    How to take a photo that can be assessed
    • Use bright, even light, such as daylight near a window. Avoid the flash and glare.
    • Hold the phone steady, tap the screen to focus, and fill the frame with the affected area.
    • Take one close-up and, if you can, one wider shot. Put a coin or your hand next to it for scale.
    • Don't use filters or edit the photo, and clean the lens first.
